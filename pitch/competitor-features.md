# Competitor features (checked 2 Nov 2026)

From public pages only. We haven't tried any of these products. "Not found" means it wasn't on the pages we read, not that the product lacks it. Vendors' pages are marketing, and comparison tables on one vendor's site are weak evidence about its rivals.

**Main finding:** line-by-line approval, AI quote drafting and voice notes are not unique to us. Nothing to switch, helping the driver understand, and the own-language view are what's left.

## Feature by feature

| Feature | Who else has it | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Link with no app | Most of the above | Expected, not a difference |
| Approve or decline each item | Workshop Software, Workshop Hub, Workshop Wrench | Not unique. Stop leading with it as a difference |
| Approving mid-job extras | OneBookPlus, and likely others | Not unique |
| AI quote from rough notes | Workshop Hub | Not unique, and Workshop Hub uses the workshop's own rates and stock |
| Voice notes | Workshop Hub, Fullbay, Tekion | Not unique. Nice to have only |
| Follow-ups on declined work | Workshop Wrench's deferred queue; reminders are common | Not unique |
| Customer views the quote in their own language | **Not found** in two searches | Possibly our clearest difference. Absence isn't proof |
| Sending through WeChat | **Not found** in one search | Not built in our app either. The link can be pasted in |
| Plain reason, urgency and part options on each line for the driver | Not found together. Workshop Software shows notes, time and cost | Likely a difference, but unconfirmed without trying them |
| Works beside any system, nothing to switch | **Not found.** Every product above is a full system, or an app inside one | Our strongest difference |

## What this means

1. **Lead with "nothing to switch", then the driver's understanding.** Don't lead with price, AI, voice or line-by-line approval.
2. **Price isn't an advantage on its own.** OneBookPlus's approval add-on can cost less than $49 in total, though it needs that system.
3. **MechanicDesk users look like a good first target.** Its public pages show no customer approval link, and our MechanicDesk workshop still approved by phone and WeChat. Workshop Software Silver users don't get approval either. Paper and WeChat workshops remain the core.
4. **The real risk isn't a big incumbent copying us.** Newer, cheaper Australian systems are already selling this as part of a full system.
