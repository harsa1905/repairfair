# Features to add

Ideas for the app, not built yet and not promised to workshops. Nothing here goes in the pitch as working until it works in the app. We haven't tried competitors' products, so don't claim they lack any of these.

| # | Feature | What it does | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | View in your own language | The driver picks a language on the same link. AI translates item names, reasons and urgency labels. Prices and approvals stay the same, and the English quote stays the record | Works for any customer, and the link can still be pasted into WeChat. Two of the five workshops we recorded run on WeChat, but no workshop asked for translation | Waiting on go |
| 2 | Voice notes while diagnosing | The mechanic speaks what they find, and the words go into the notes box to edit. Save the text, not the recording | Suits a mechanic at the car. No workshop asked for it | Waiting on go, after 1 |
| 3 | Separate add-ons view | Add-ons the garage recommends sit in their own clean section of the quote, apart from the work the car needs | Keeps the needed repair clear, so an add-on doesn't read like required work | Requested by Sameep, 2 Nov 2026 |
| 4 | Extras-only approval | One photo, a price and a link, with no full quote | Easiest way in for a workshop that won't change its system. Targets the $60 problem | Idea |
| 5 | Approval receipt | A one-page record for both sides of what was shown, what was approved and when | Answers "I never agreed to that". Two of ten workshops had lost money on extras | Idea |
