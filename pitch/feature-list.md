# Features to add

Ideas for the app, not promised to workshops. Nothing here goes in the pitch as working until it works in the app. We haven't tried competitors' products, so don't claim they lack any of these.

| # | Feature | What it does | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | View in your own language | The driver picks a language on the same link. AI translates item names, reasons and urgency labels. Prices and approvals stay the same, and the English quote stays the record | Works for any customer, and the link can still be pasted into WeChat. Two of the five workshops we recorded run on WeChat, but no workshop asked for translation | Built on `app-dev`. Chinese and Vietnamese wording not checked by a fluent speaker |
| 2 | Voice notes while diagnosing | The mechanic speaks what they find, and the words go into the notes box to edit. Save the text, not the recording | Suits a mechanic at the car. No workshop asked for it, and Workshop Hub already has it, so it's nice to have, not a difference | Built on `app-dev`. Not tested on a real microphone |
| 3 | Separate add-ons view | Add-ons the garage recommends sit in their own clean section of the quote, apart from the work the car needs | Keeps the needed repair clear, so an add-on doesn't read like required work | Built on `app-dev` |
| 4 | Extras-only approval | One photo, a price and a link, with no full quote | Easiest way in for a workshop that won't change its system. Targets the $60 problem | Idea |
| 5 | Approval receipt | A one-page record for both sides of what the driver saw, including the language, reasons and prices, what they approved and when | Answers "I never agreed to that". Two of ten workshops had lost money on extras. Don't claim legal strength without advice | Idea |
| 6 | Built-in invoicing | The approved quote becomes the invoice, with no retyping. Only approved lines appear | Lets a paper workshop run the whole job here | Built on `app-dev`. Sample ABN, not checked against tax invoice rules |
| 7 | Trusted garage network | With consent, approval records show which garages quote clearly. Free driver tools: car history, finding trusted garages, a real fair-price check, verified reviews | Our long-term difference. Drivers stay free, and workshops can't pay for a better ranking | Later |
